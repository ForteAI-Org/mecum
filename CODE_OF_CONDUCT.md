# Code of Conduct

Everyone participating in Mecum, including maintainers, must treat others with
respect. This applies in project-managed spaces and when representing the project.

- Critique ideas and implementations; explain disagreements constructively.
- Welcome questions and different backgrounds. Respect personal boundaries.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, share private information without consent,
  or retaliate against someone who reports a concern.
- Report concerns privately. Uninvolved administrators review reports and appeals.

**Reporting contact: `CONDUCT_CONTACT` (placeholder).** Until a monitored contact
is published, use an existing private channel with a ForteAI-Org administrator.
Choose another administrator if that person is involved in the concern.

## Report a concern

Describe what happened, with relevant links and dates. Share only the personal
information needed to assess it; do not discuss the report in the affected issue
or pull request. Unwanted sexual attention and deliberate intimidation are also
unacceptable, whether directed at contributors or maintainers.

## Review and outcomes

Administrators should review the evidence, restrict access to report details and
explain resulting actions to affected people. Anyone involved must step aside
from deciding the outcome.

Depending on severity and repeated behavior, responses may include a request to
change behavior, content removal, a warning or temporary or permanent restrictions.
Affected people may request a private review by an uninvolved administrator.

Before public launch, maintainers must replace `CONDUCT_CONTACT`, name the people
handling reports and verify private delivery. The placeholder is not an active contact.
Do not report software vulnerabilities in public issues or pull requests.
